# 🏦 Banking Analytics Dashboard | Microsoft Power BI

### Turning Banking Data into Actionable Business Insights

## 📌 Project Overview

An end-to-end banking analytics project developed using **Microsoft Power BI** to analyze customer behavior, financial transactions, loan performance, and customer support operations.

The project uses data transformation, data modeling, DAX calculations, and interactive visualizations to identify business challenges and support informed decision-making.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze customer engagement and account activity.
- Monitor transaction performance and financial balances.
- Evaluate loan distribution and interest rates.
- Measure customer support efficiency.
- Identify business opportunities through data-driven insights.

## ⚙️ Project Workflow

### 1. Data Understanding & Exploration
- Explored the available banking datasets.
- Identified relevant tables, columns, and attributes.
- Defined business questions and KPIs.
- Understood the relationships between banking entities.

### 2. Data Cleaning & Transformation – Power Query
- Inspected data quality and consistency.
- Checked missing values and duplicate records.
- Corrected data types and formatting.
- Prepared datasets for analytical modeling.

### 3. Data Modeling
- Established relationships between tables.
- Reviewed primary and foreign key fields.
- Evaluated relationship cardinality.
- Configured filter directions.
- Structured the model to support accurate calculations.

### 4. DAX & KPI Development
Developed analytical measures to track:
- Total customers and accounts.
- Active and inactive accounts.
- Transaction volume and value.
- Total customer balances.
- Total loan amounts.
- Average interest rates.
- Customer support resolution rates.

### 5. Dashboard Development

Created four interactive dashboard pages:

#### 📊 Overview
- 5K Customers
- 20K Transactions
- $100.1M Transaction Value
- $249.4M Total Balance
- $616.7M Total Loans

#### 👥 Customer Analysis
- 5K Accounts
- 3.171K Account Holders
- 3.097K Active Accounts
- 1.903K Inactive Accounts
- Account distribution and customer transaction behavior.

#### 💰 Loans Analysis
- 3K Loans
- 7.48% Average Interest Rate
- $500K Highest Loan
- $1K Lowest Loan
- Loan distribution and maturity analysis.

#### 🎧 Customer Support
- 3K Total Issues
- 1.479K Issues Solved
- 49.30% Resolution Rate
- Customer issue categories and geographic distribution.

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

| Insight | Business implication |
|---|---|
| 38.06% inactive accounts | Opportunity to improve customer retention |
| 49.30% issue resolution rate | Need to improve customer support efficiency |
| $616.7M loan portfolio | Importance of monitoring lending exposure |
| 775 transaction disputes | Opportunity to investigate recurring complaints |
| Checking accounts have the highest average balance | Opportunity to optimize account strategies |

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Introduce targeted customer reactivation campaigns.
- Improve complaint management and ticket prioritization.
- Investigate recurring transaction disputes.
- Monitor loan repayment and maturity patterns.
- Segment customers based on transaction behavior.

- **DAX:** KPI development and analytical calculations.
- **Data Modeling:** Relationships and analytical model design.
